K-Means Clustering: The House of Knowledge

Master K-Means — from WCSS minimization and Lloyd's Expectation-Maximization steps to K-Means++ seeding, Silhouette scoring, and failure modes.

💡

K-Means Clustering for Beginners (Explain Like I'm 5)

Imagine opening $K=3$ coffee shops in a city. You want to place the 3 shops (the Centroids) so that every person walks the shortest possible distance to their nearest shop! K-Means automatically discovers natural group centers through a 2-step dance that repeats until equilibrium!

📍 1. Assign Points

Every person (data point) joins whichever of the $K$ shop centroids is closest to them.

🚶‍♂️ 2. Move Centroids

Each shop moves to the exact mathematical center (average position) of all citizens currently in its group.

📐 3. Repeat to Convergence

Repeat steps 1 & 2 until centroid positions stop shifting. Total walking distance (WCSS / Inertia) is minimized!

🎯

1. Unsupervised Goal

K-Means partitions \(n\) unlabeled observations into \(K\) clusters \(C = \{C_1, C_2, \dots, C_K\}\) where each point belongs to the cluster with the nearest mean centroid \(\mu_k\).

📐

2. WCSS / Inertia Objective

Minimizes the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS), representing total intra-cluster variance:

$$J = \sum_{k=1}^{K}\sum_{x_i \in C_k}\|x_i - \mu_k\|^2$$

where \(\mu_k = \frac{1}{|C_k|}\sum_{x_i \in C_k} x_i\) is the mean vector of cluster \(C_k\).

⚡

3. Lloyd's EM Algorithm

Alternates two steps until centroids stop changing (convergence):

$$\text{1. Assign: } c_i = \arg\min_k \|x_i - \mu_k\|^2$$
$$\text{2. Update: } \mu_k = \frac{1}{|C_k|}\sum_{x_i \in C_k} x_i$$
🚀

4. K-Means++ Initialization

Random seeding can get trapped in bad local minima. K-Means++ seeds centroids far apart by selecting subsequent centroids with probability proportional to squared distance:

$$P(x) = \frac{D(x)^2}{\sum_{x' \in X} D(x')^2}$$

Guarantees an \(\mathcal{O}(\log K)\) approximation ratio to the optimal solution.

📉

5. The Elbow Method

As \(K\) increases, WCSS monotonically decreases. We plot WCSS vs \(K\) and choose the elbow point where marginal gain drops off sharply:

$$\text{Elbow} = \arg\max_K \bigl| \Delta^2 J(K) \bigr|$$
📊

6. Silhouette Coefficient

Quantifies how well-separated clusters are without knowing ground truth labels:

$$s(i) = \frac{b(i) - a(i)}{\max(a(i), b(i))}$$

\(a(i)\) = mean intra-cluster distance; \(b(i)\) = mean nearest-cluster distance. \(s(i) \in [-1, 1]\); higher is better.
