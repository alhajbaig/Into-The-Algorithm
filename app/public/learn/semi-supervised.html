01 — Theory & Foundations

House of Knowledge

Mathematical formulations, confidence thresholding, and production guidelines for semi-supervised self-training.

🎯

1. Supervised Seed Objective

Initial teacher model trains on small labeled dataset \(\mathcal{D}_L = \{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^{N_L}\) using standard Cross-Entropy loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_L = -\frac{1}{N_L} \sum_{i=1}^{N_L} \sum_{k=1}^K y_{i,k} \log f_\theta(x_i)_k$$
⚡

2. Confidence Thresholding (\(\tau\))

Unlabeled samples \(\mathcal{D}_U\) receive pseudo-labels \(\hat{y}_u = \arg\max_k f_\theta(x_u)_k\) only if prediction confidence exceeds threshold \(\tau\):

$$\mathbb{I}(\max_k f_\theta(x_u)_k \ge \tau) \cdot \text{CrossEntropy}(f_\theta(x_u), \hat{y}_u)$$
🔄

3. Iterative Bootstrapping

Pseudo-labeled samples are augmented into training set \(\mathcal{D}_{new} = \mathcal{D}_L \cup \mathcal{D}_{pseudo}\) for student retraining across rounds:

$$\theta^{(t+1)} = \arg\min_\theta \left( \mathcal{L}_L(\theta) + \lambda_u \mathcal{L}_{pseudo}(\theta) \right)$$
🛡️

4. Entropy Minimization & Regularization

Encourages low-entropy decision boundaries passing through sparse data regions, preventing confirmation bias and drift:

$$\mathcal{H}(p) = -\sum_{k=1}^K p_k \log p_k \to 0$$

💡 Interview & Production Guidelines

Q: What causes Confirmation Bias in Pseudo-Labeling?

If the seed model makes confident misclassifications early on, pseudo-labeling reinforces false beliefs, driving the student model into error spirals. Mitigation: High confidence thresholds (\(\tau \ge 0.95\)), MixUp, or Teacher-Student Consistency (FixMatch).

Q: How to balance Class Imbalance in Pseudo-Labels?

Majority classes often dominate high-confidence predictions. Use dynamic class-wise thresholds \(\tau_c\) or temperature scaling to prevent class collapse.

📊 Algorithmic Comparison: Self-Training vs Label Propagation vs SGAN

Algorithm Assumption / Mechanics Scalability Best Used When
Self-Training High-confidence predictions are true targets \(\mathcal{O}(N)\) Very High Vast unlabeled datasets with strong deep backbone
Label Propagation Graph manifold smoothness (RBF similarity) \(\mathcal{O}(N^2)\) Medium Structured low-dimensional manifold datasets
Semi-Supervised GAN Discriminator predicts \(K+1\) classes (K real + Fake) High (requires GPU) Complex image domains with generative priors
