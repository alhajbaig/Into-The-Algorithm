Generative Semi-Supervised Learning

SEMI-SUPERVISED GANs (SGAN)

Transform standard binary discriminators into (K+1)-class classifiers, leveraging adversarial generator manifolds to boost classification accuracy from minimal seed data.

01 — Theory & Foundations

House of Knowledge

K+1 categorical objectives, feature matching, and joint supervised-unsupervised adversarial losses.

🎯

1. (K+1) Discriminator Formulation

Discriminator outputs Softmax probabilities over \(K\) real categories plus an extra \((K+1)\)-th class for generated fake samples:

$$P_D(y = k \mid x) = \frac{\exp(l_k)}{\sum_{j=1}^{K+1} \exp(l_j)}$$
⚡

2. Supervised Loss (\(\mathcal{L}_{supervised}\))

Standard categorical cross-entropy evaluated exclusively over the sparse labeled seed samples \((x, y) \sim p_{data}\):

$$\mathcal{L}_{supervised} = -\mathbb{E}_{(x, y) \sim p_{data}} \left[ \log P_D(y \mid x, y \le K) \right]$$
🔄

3. Unsupervised Loss (\(\mathcal{L}_{unsupervised}\))

Binary real-vs-fake objective over all unlabeled samples \(x \sim p_{data}\) vs generated samples \(G(z)\):

$$\mathcal{L}_{unsupervised} = -\mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_{data}} [\log(1 - P_D(y = K+1 \mid x))] - \mathbb{E}_{z \sim p_z} [\log P_D(y = K+1 \mid G(z))]$$
🔮

4. Feature Matching Generator Objective

Generator minimizes distance between expected intermediate discriminator feature representations \(\mathbf{f}(x)\):

$$\mathcal{L}_G = \left\| \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_{data}} [\mathbf{f}(x)] - \mathbb{E}_{z \sim p_z} [\mathbf{f}(G(z))] \right\|_2^2$$

💡 Interview & Production Guidelines

Q: Why does a Generative Adversarial prior improve classification?

The Generator forces the Discriminator to learn decision boundaries that lie strictly outside the true data manifold. Fake samples push class decision boundaries into sparse, low-density regions.

Q: How to prevent Discriminator Collapse in SGAN?

Use Feature Matching loss for Generator optimization instead of classic minimax cross-entropy, and apply spectral normalization to Discriminator layers.
