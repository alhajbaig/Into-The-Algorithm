01 — Theory & Foundations

House of Knowledge

NT-Xent InfoNCE loss, temperature scaling \(\tau\), momentum queues, and alignment vs uniformity properties.

🎯

1. NT-Xent / InfoNCE Loss

Evaluates categorical cross-entropy over cosine similarities \(\text{sim}(u, v) = \frac{u^T v}{\|u\| \|v\|}\) across positive and negative pairs:

$$\ell_{i, j} = -\log \frac{\exp(\text{sim}(z_i, z_j) / \tau)}{\sum_{k=1}^{2N} \mathbb{I}_{[k \ne i]} \exp(\text{sim}(z_i, z_k) / \tau)}$$
⚡

2. Temperature Hyperparameter (\(\tau\))

Controls hardness of negative mining. Lower \(\tau\) emphasizes nearest hard negative repulsions, sharpening hypersphere cluster boundaries:

$$\tau \in [0.05, 0.5] \implies \text{Gradient Weight} \propto \exp(\text{sim}/\tau)$$
🔄

3. MoCo Momentum Queue (\(K\))

Decouples dictionary size from batch size using a FIFO memory queue \(K=65,536\) updated via exponential moving average (\(m=0.999\)):

$$\theta_k \leftarrow m \theta_k + (1 - m) \theta_q$$
🛡️

4. Alignment & Uniformity

Representation quality is governed by positive pair closeness (Alignment) and uniform distribution over unit sphere \(\mathbb{S}^d\) (Uniformity):

$$\mathcal{L}_{align} = \mathbb{E} \|z_i - z_j\|^2, \quad \mathcal{L}_{uniform} = \log \mathbb{E} \exp(-2\|z_i - z_k\|^2)$$

💡 Interview & Production Guidelines

Q: Why is Non-Linear Projection Head \(g(z)\) critical?

SimCLR drops projection head \(g(z)\) during downstream transfer! The projection head discards fine-grained transformation variance, allowing encoder \(f(x)\) to retain richer features.

Q: Why does SimCLR require gigantic batch sizes (4096)?

InfoNCE requires many negative keys to prevent representation collapse. MoCo circumvents large batch size requirements by maintaining an explicit dynamic memory queue.
