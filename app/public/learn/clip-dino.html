01 — Theory & Foundations

House of Knowledge

Symmetric cross-entropy, joint vision-text projections, and DINO v2 student-teacher centering & sharpening.

🎯

1. CLIP Symmetric Cross-Entropy

Calculates dual cross-entropy loss over cosine matrix \(\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{I} \cdot \mathbf{T}^T \cdot e^\tau\) across image and text batches:

$$\mathcal{L}_{CLIP} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \mathcal{L}_{img \to text} + \mathcal{L}_{text \to img} \right)$$
⚡

2. Zero-Shot Prompting (\(W_{text}\))

Classifies images without fine-tuning by comparing image embeddings \(z_I\) against candidate text prompt embeddings \(z_T = \text{TextEnc}("A photo of a \{class\}")\):

$$\hat{y} = \arg\max_k \cos(z_I, z_{T, k})$$
🔄

3. DINO v2 Self-Distillation

Student network \(g_{\theta_s}\) predicts Teacher network \(g_{\theta_t}\) probability distribution via EMA parameter updates \(\theta_t \leftarrow m \theta_t + (1-m) \theta_s\):

$$\min_{\theta_s} H(P_t(x), P_s(x)) = -\sum P_t(x) \log P_s(x)$$
🛡️

4. Centering & Sharpening

Prevents collapse in self-distillation by subtracting dynamic center \(c\) (Centering) and scaling temperature \(\tau_t < \tau_s\) (Sharpening):

$$P_t(x) = \text{Softmax}\left(\frac{g_{\theta_t}(x) - c}{\tau_t}\right)$$

💡 Interview & Production Guidelines

Q: Why does DINO v2 produce superior dense features vs CLIP?

CLIP optimizes global image-level text alignment, losing fine-grained spatial geometry. DINO v2 applies patch-level self-distillation and Sinkhorn-Knopp centering, producing state-of-the-art dense features for segmentation and depth estimation.

Q: How to prevent Collapse in Label-Free Self-Distillation?

DINO uses two complementary mechanisms: Centering (prevents one dimension from dominating) and Sharpening (prevents uniform distribution collapse).
